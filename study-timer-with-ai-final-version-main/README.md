# study-timer-with-ai-final-version

Excited to share what I’ve been building: Grove 🌿 — a gamified 3D study garden and AI workspace designed to make deep work effortless and engaging.
​Studying usually means juggling 5 different apps: a Pomodoro timer, a flashcard app, an AI tutor, a PDF summarizer, and a distraction tracker. Grove brings all of them under one roof

3D Study Garden & Focus Timer
Run a focus session and watch a glowing seed plant and grow in 3D in real-time. Comes with built-in ambient audio like Rainy Lo-fi to keep you in the zone. 

 Notes & Quiz Generator
Feed it PDFs, lecture transcripts, or YouTube video links. Grove instantly distills them into summaries, AI study briefs, and interactive quizzes

Auto Flashcard Deck Forge
Paste raw study material to automatically extract key terms and create cloze/definition review decks. 

AI Doubt Solver
Combines on-device step-by-step equation solving with a Gemini Cloud Tutor for deep explanations across math, physics, chemistry, and more

Distraction Audit
Calculate your exact attention cost. Track phone pickups, context switching, and social media time to see how many hours/days you lose each year — and what reclaiming them buys you.

Earn XP for every focus session, doubt asked, and deck reviewed. Keep daily streaks alive and watch your study garden level up!
